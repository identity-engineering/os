# Ecosystem vision (orientation, not v0 product claim)

Locked orientation 29.07.2026

## One-sentence stance

IE OS is an **open Identity-Geometry standard + local-first runtime** on which a collective-intelligence ecosystem *may* grow — not a mandate to build the next social network.

## Three layers we keep distinct

| Layer | Meaning | What we build now | What we do not claim now |
|-------|---------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| **1. Standard / Contracts** | Registry, Interaction Signal, Surface ops, foreign-estimate zone, estimate request/inbox, Ownership and privacy defaults | Schemas, docs, deterministic local apply | "This replaces LinkedIn / X / Moltbook" |
| **2. Installable Runtime** | Free local OS + optional managed Surface (Pro) | Surface Runtime, CLI path, hooks, storage tiers | A mandatory global graph or attention feed |
| **3. Ecosystem (horizon)** | Other systems speak the contracts; humans, agents, orgs, Physical AI, and existing networks dock in; collective gravitational fields emerge | Keep contracts open and implementable; bridges later | That we own or operate the entire social layer |

## Why the social-network analogy is useful — and dangerous

**Useful**
- Registry ≈ local follow / recognition list
- Accept signal ≈ connection accept
- Estimate request ≈ endorsement / rating request with inbox agency
- Foreign-estimate zone ≈ bounded place where others' views of me land

**Dangerous if over-identified**
- Social products optimise for attention, engagement, content ranking
- IE OS optimises for relative Mass, volume, tension, Stem ownership, causal entropy
- Confusing the two pulls implementation toward feeds, virality, and platform lock-in

Hold the analogy as a **structural map**, not as product identity.

## Relation to existing networks

- **Moltbook** and similar agent forums: content and community among agents. Complementary. IE contracts can sit beside them (identity geometry layer), not replace their post/upvote game.
- **Classical social (LinkedIn, X, …)**: could *speak* IE signals or host a surface without IE becoming their clone.
- **Strategy**: prefer **extension and bridging** over replacement. Open contracts make that possible.

## What "collective intelligence ecosystem" means here

Not a single company graph.

A field in which many Identities (human, runtime, org, idea, Physical AI) maintain local Registries, exchange minimal signals and optional estimates under Ownership defaults, and derive relative Mass / volume / tension — so that collective curvature and alignment become *measurable and regulable* rather than only narrative.

That field can emerge if:

1. Contracts stay stable and honest about asymptotics
2. Local install remains viable without a platform account
3. Managed Pro is optional convenience, not the only mode
4. Refusal-of-Control and human approval for critical surface changes remain structural

## Practical rules for this repo and public wording

1. **Docs and issues**: prefer "open contracts + local runtime first; ecosystem may grow".
2. **Public positioning**: name the social analogy, state the telos difference sharply, avoid platform rhetoric before the bidirectional sensor works.
3. **Architecture decisions**: when choosing between "more platform" and "clearer contract", prefer the contract.
4. **Vision storage**: this file is the durable orientation note inside `os`. Strategic echoes may appear in Notion Vision Manifest / History as Insight, not as a premature product roadmap item.

## Non-goals (explicit)

- Building a global attention feed as core product
- Requiring every Identity to rate every other Identity
- Auto-answering estimate requests
- Claiming empirical "collective intelligence platform" status before dogfood and open contracts exist
