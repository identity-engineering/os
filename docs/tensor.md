# Living Tensor (derived from Registry)

There is no separate persistent Tensor file.

The high-dimensional, open, contentful dimensional space that lives inside each Registry entry *is* the Tensor.

## Core statements (locked 27.07.2026)

1. All numbers, dimensions and distances reside in the Registry.
2. Tension is always dependent on the whole set of registered Identities → it is dynamic and emergent.
3. The list of dimensions is not static. It is Identity-specific and extensible.
4. When a new dimension is discovered for one Identity, the observer can (ownership-controlled) analyse whether it is also relevant for other already known Identities.
5. Dimensions are not the abstract framework primitives (Mass, Vision, …). They are richer, contentful vector spaces that describe the actual substance, character and worldly classification of an Identity — the material science of Mass.
6. The precise physical analog of how material / dimensional differences produce Curvature of the possibility space is still an open requirement of the Identity Engineering tension experience.
7. Distance estimation on every dimension always carries its own confidence.
8. This structure gives the Identity a clear, expandable world-view. New Identities can introduce new dimensions that open new perspectives on existing ones. Questions as Probes become the instruments that measure and deliberately extend this experienced curved space.

## Geometry of the space (locked 27.07.2026)

- The Possibility Space on the Identity level is **not 3-dimensional**. It is the infinite-dimensional space whose basis is the open, growing set of all discovered dimensions (the distinct sum across the entire Registry).
- Each Identity is an **alloy**: a vector (or distribution) over that basis. The degree of expression on each dimension is the corresponding potential mass component along that axis.
- **Mass** is the (relative, confidence- and interaction-depth-weighted) **density of this alloy**. This stays consistent with the framework definition of Mass as density / substance depth, not volume.
- **Volume** candidate: the number (or weighted count) of interacting Identities that estimate / orbit me. They are the ones who sample my dimensions and thereby contribute to the emergent picture of my density.
- Self-Mass is never self-declared. It emerges from the Mass estimates that the surrounding Identities return about me, weighted by their own Mass and by interaction depth. I may also hold my own estimates of the surrounding; the signal I receive from them is the other half of the information.
- Density parameters may still need an explicit depth / intensity factor per dimension or overall (open question).
- With the above, an Identity has rich information about its own and others' densities and Masses, and a workable definition of volume.

## Implications

- Registry = primary store (including the open dimensional alloys)
- Tensor = geometric reading of the Registry (live)
- Tension = aggregation over the Registry (live)
- Curvature = later derived quantity from the distribution of these mass-densities + multi-dimensional distances
- What I estimate vs. what I receive via signal remains a clean separation that the interaction contract must respect

This decision keeps the OS minimal, relative, and ownership-preserving while giving maximum geometric power to the observing Identity.
